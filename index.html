About Me

Turning Complex Problems into Elegant Solutions

I'm an AI/ML Engineer with a unique blend of biological sciences and manufacturing engineering background from BITS Pilani. This interdisciplinary education gives me a distinctive perspective on solving complex problems.

My journey started in research at the intersection of biology, material science, and AI/ML, working on computational discovery of battery materials and mining biological literature using NLP and deep learning.

From there, I gained industry experience across diverse domains: healthcare (breast cancer classification at APS Labs), digital advertising (optimizing ad engagement at Rocketium), and FMCG at Hindustan Unilever Ltd. where I collaborated with Deloitte consultants on ML-driven process optimization. Currently at Mira, Aroha Labs, I build AI agents and event-driven microservices on AWS, powering intelligent systems with LangChain, vector databases, and RAG architectures.
