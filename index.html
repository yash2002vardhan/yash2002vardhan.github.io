About

I'm an engineer working at the intersection of agentic AI and real, deployed systems.

Most of my work lives where language models meet production. I build the pieces that make agents genuinely useful: custom memory so they remember, hybrid retrieval so they reason over the right context, MCP-based tool use so they can act, and the event-driven infrastructure that keeps it all dependable at scale. What I enjoy most is owning the full arc, from a rough research question to a microservice running quietly in production. Benchmark · Quantization 2026

quant_cliff

A study of how agentic tool use degrades under quantization: 18 configs, two Qwen3 sizes, 193 tasks, with schema validity, tool selection and argument correctness each scored deterministically and no LLM judge anywhere. The finding is that the cliff is model-dependent, not precision-dependent. At Q3_K_M the 1.7B loses 23.8 points of task success while the 4B moves 0.5, yet perplexity reports the same 21% degradation for both. Domain-matched imatrix calibration wins 18.7 of those points back at zero size cost.

  • Quantization
  • Tool Use
  • Evaluation

Benchmark · Python 2026

JANUS

An anticipatory-memory benchmark scoring both halves of memory: recall (finding the right past fact, auto-graded against known answers, no AI judge) and prefetch (having the next-needed context ready before it's asked, graded against a computable Bayes-optimal ceiling). Data is generated, not borrowed, so every answer and ceiling is known exactly.
